How to Interpret These Results

These are reported outcomes for this campaign, not guaranteed benchmarks or forecasts. Traffic, ranking, enquiry, and technical measures describe different aspects of performance and should not be compared as if they were equivalent. A future campaign should be assessed against its own baseline, measurement setup, market, and business goals.

Practical Takeaway

For other wordpress website businesses considering WordPress SEO, this case shows which measures are worth tracking from day one: search visibility, clicks, and the business action that matters most, such as enquiries, sales, or sign-ups. The right priorities depend on the business's starting position, target audience, and objectives. Learn more about Technical SEO services.
