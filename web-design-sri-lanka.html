Choosing the Best Web Designing Company in Sri Lanka

There are hundreds of web design companies in Sri Lanka, from freelancers in Colombo to large agencies. The fastest way to compare them is to open their live projects on your phone. If a portfolio site is slow, hard to tap or missing a clear call to action, the same will happen to yours.

Before you hire, ask four questions:

  • Can I see live websites, not just mock-ups? Every project above links to a working site.
  • Is SEO included? A new site should launch with a proper heading structure, meta tags, schema and a sitemap. Fixing it later costs more.
  • Who owns the domain and hosting? They should be registered in your business name.
  • What happens after launch? Find out who handles updates, backups and security fixes.

Common Questions

Web Design Sri Lanka FAQs

How much does web design cost in Sri Lanka?

A 5-page static website without a backend costs LKR 10,000, and a dynamic e-commerce website with an admin dashboard costs LKR 25,000. Both include free hosting and a free SSL certificate for the first year, with hosting renewal at LKR 5,000 per year from the second year. The domain name is provided by the client. Larger custom websites are quoted separately.

How do I choose the best web designing company in Sri Lanka?

Look at live projects on both desktop and mobile, check page speed, ask who owns the domain and hosting, and confirm the site is built with on-page SEO, schema markup and Google Search Console from day one. A good web design company in Sri Lanka will show real, working websites rather than mock-ups.

How long does it take to design a website?

A starter website typically takes 1 to 2 weeks, a full business website 3 to 4 weeks, and an e-commerce store 4 to 8 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly content such as text, photos and product details is ready.

Will my new website be SEO friendly and rank on Google?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first with clean headings, fast-loading images, structured data, an XML sitemap and Search Console setup. Ranking for competitive keywords still needs ongoing content and link building, which can be added as an SEO plan.

Can you build a Sinhala or Tamil website?

Yes. Bilingual Sinhala/English websites with a language switch are supported, as shown in the Ramadoothappan Sami project in this portfolio, with correct Unicode fonts and hreflang-ready structure.

Should I take a web design course in Sri Lanka or hire a designer?

A web design course in Sri Lanka is a good choice if you want web design as a career skill. If you need a business website that brings in leads quickly, hiring an experienced designer is usually faster and cheaper overall, because speed, SEO and conversion design take years of practice to get right.
