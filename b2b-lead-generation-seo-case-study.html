Campaign Summary

Over seven months, organic clicks increased by 76%, and commercial keyword rankings expanded from 145 to 310. Qualified leads from organic search increased by 69%, supported by improved service pages and search-intent targeting.

Campaign Scope

This anonymized B2B Lead Generation campaign applied B2B SEO for Organic search, with a reported timeframe of 7 months.

The Objective

The B2B business needed organic search to produce qualified leads for its sales pipeline, not just traffic.

What Was Done

01Service-page improvements

Service pages were improved so they answered what B2B buyers need to know before making contact.

02Search-intent targeting

Pages were matched to the intent behind commercial searches, and commercial keyword rankings grew from 145 to 310.

03Qualified lead tracking

Leads from organic search were assessed for quality, and qualified organic leads rose by 69%.
