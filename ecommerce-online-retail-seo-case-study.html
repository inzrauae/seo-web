Campaign Summary

Over six months, organic clicks increased by 112%, and commercial keyword rankings expanded from 240 to 510. Organic product-page visits grew by 96%, while organic revenue increased by 41% during the reporting period.

Campaign Scope

This anonymized Online Retail campaign applied eCommerce SEO for Organic search, with a reported timeframe of 6 months.

The Objective

The online retailer needed organic search to drive sales, not only visits. The campaign focused on commercial keywords and the product pages that convert.

What Was Done

01Commercial keyword expansion

Category and product pages were aligned with buying-intent searches, and commercial keyword rankings grew from 240 to 510.

02Product-page visibility

Product pages were treated as the main landing pages for organic search, and organic product-page visits grew by 96%.

03Revenue measurement

Organic revenue was tracked through the reporting period so the effect of SEO could be read in sales terms, not just traffic.
