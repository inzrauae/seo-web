Campaign Summary

Over eight months, organic clicks increased by 88%, and ranking property-related keywords expanded from 190 to 405. Organic lead submissions grew by 46%, with improved visibility for property searches across targeted locations.

Campaign Scope

This anonymized Real Estate campaign applied Real Estate SEO for Targeted locations, with a reported timeframe of 8 months.

The Objective

The real estate website needed to capture property searches across its target locations and turn them into lead submissions.

What Was Done

Pages were aligned with property-related searches, and ranking keywords grew from 190 to 405.

02Location visibility

Visibility was built for property searches across each targeted location.

How to Interpret These Results

These are reported outcomes for this campaign, not guaranteed benchmarks or forecasts. Traffic, ranking, enquiry, and technical measures describe different aspects of performance and should not be compared as if they were equivalent. A future campaign should be assessed against its own baseline, measurement setup, market, and business goals.

Practical Takeaway

For other real estate businesses considering Real Estate SEO, this case shows which measures are worth tracking from day one: search visibility, clicks, and the business action that matters most, such as enquiries, sales, or sign-ups. The right priorities depend on the business's starting position, target audience, and objectives. Learn more about SEO services.
