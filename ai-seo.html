Common Questions

AI SEO FAQs

What is AI SEO (GEO)?

AI SEO, also called Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), is the practice of optimizing content so it gets discovered, understood, and cited by AI-powered search and answer engines like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity, alongside traditional search engines like Google.

How is AI SEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking in a list of blue links, while AI SEO focuses on being accurately summarized and cited within AI-generated answers, which requires clearer structure, verifiable facts, and stronger topical authority.

Why does AI SEO matter for Sri Lankan businesses in 2025?

More users are now researching products and services through AI assistants before ever visiting a website, so businesses that aren't optimized for AI discovery risk losing visibility even if they rank well in traditional Google search.

What are the key components of a strong AI SEO strategy?

Effective AI SEO combines clear, factual content structure, complete schema markup, strong E-E-A-T signals, and traditional technical and on-page SEO fundamentals, since AI engines still rely heavily on well-optimized, crawlable websites as their source data.
