Campaign Summary

The technical SEO campaign resolved 47 identified issues, including broken internal links, canonicalisation conflicts, and indexing problems. Indexed priority pages increased by 38%, and organic clicks grew by 54% over five months.

Campaign Scope

This anonymized Business Website campaign applied Technical SEO for Organic search, with a reported timeframe of 5 months.

The Objective

The business website had technical problems that stopped important pages from being crawled and indexed correctly. Fixing that foundation came before any other SEO work.

What Was Done

01Technical audit and issue list

The site was audited and 47 specific issues were identified and resolved.

02Broken internal links

Broken internal links were fixed so users and search engines could reach priority pages without dead ends.

03Canonicalisation and indexing

Canonical conflicts and indexing problems were corrected so search engines indexed the intended version of each priority page. Indexed priority pages increased by 38%.

How to Interpret These Results

These are reported outcomes for this campaign, not guaranteed benchmarks or forecasts. Traffic, ranking, enquiry, and technical measures describe different aspects of performance and should not be compared as if they were equivalent. A future campaign should be assessed against its own baseline, measurement setup, market, and business goals.

Practical Takeaway

For other business website businesses considering Technical SEO, this case shows which measures are worth tracking from day one: search visibility, clicks, and the business action that matters most, such as enquiries, sales, or sign-ups. The right priorities depend on the business's starting position, target audience, and objectives. Learn more about Technical SEO services.
